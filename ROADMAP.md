2. **RISAT-1A (EOS-04) C-band Hybrid Polarimetry:**
   - Process Compact Polarimetry (CP) Stokes parameters ($S_0, S_1, S_2, S_3$) to derive degree of polarization ($m$) and relative phase ($\delta$).

   - Transition the 1D Richards PDE finite-difference kernel to a multi-threaded PyTorch CUDA 2D/3D vadose zone tensor solver.
