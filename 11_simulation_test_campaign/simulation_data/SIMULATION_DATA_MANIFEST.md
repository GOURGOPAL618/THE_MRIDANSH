## 4. Brutally Honest Technical Disclosures ("Ek Ek Dhappa")

1. **Dimensional Compression in Trajectory Arrays:**
   * The binary arrays (`.npy`) serialize **1D spatial average column moisture** rather than full 3D discretized finite-difference grids to preserve low memory footprint (~16 KB vs ~120 MB). Spatial heterogeneity within the 0--100 cm profile is collapsed into effective skin (0--5 cm) and root-zone (5--100 cm) layers.
2. **Truncation of Tractor Rutting Ceiling:**
   * In `Q4_liquefaction_250_trajectories.npy`, rut depths are hard-clamped at $z_{\text{rut}} = 35.0\,\text{cm}$ representing the physical clearance limit where the tractor undercarriage/differential housing contacts ground level ("beached chassis"). The model does not simulate dynamic mud fluidization or chassis towing forces beyond beaching.
3. **Zero Richards Mass Drift Mechanism:**
   * The recorded mass drift of $0.000000\,\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$ is achieved by an algebraic water balance formulation combined with post-hoc clipping at porosity $\theta_s$. In cases of extreme deluge, unabsorbed surface water is routed directly to surface runoff ($Q_{\text{runoff}}$) without solving 2D hydrodynamic overland flow waves.
