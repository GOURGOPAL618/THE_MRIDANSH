\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{P}{lanetary} scale remote sensing data assimilation requires rigorous invariant preservation across combined agricultural and geotechnical domains. The \texttt{AETHER-MRID1607X} core engine bridges spaceborne multi-spectral optical reflectance (Sentinel-2 MSI), synthetic aperture radar backscatter (Sentinel-1 C-band SAR), and one-dimensional vadose hydrodynamic propagation (Richards PDE with Van Genuchten retention parameters).

To validate system robustness prior to TRL-7 operational flight deployment, a continuous 1,000-trial Monte Carlo stress campaign was executed at the Jagannath Command Center (JCC) Campus 2 (RS-SDA Division).

\section{Verification Architecture and Flight Invariants}
Following RTCA DO-178C Level-A principles, every simulated trial must strictly conform to bounded flight invariants:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{INV-HYDRO-01 (Porosity Ceiling):} $\theta(t) \le \theta_{\text{sat}}$ to preclude non-physical supersaturation in rigid soil matrices.
    \item \textbf{INV-HYDRO-02 (Residual Floor \& Monotonicity):} $\theta(t) \ge \theta_{\text{res}}$ and $d\theta/dt \le 0$ under zero precipitation.
    \item \textbf{INV-HYDRO-03 (Mass Conservation Continuity):} Total flux loss $\Delta S$ matches combined actual evapotranspiration ($ET_a$) and deep drainage ($D_{\text{deep}}$), maintaining $| \text{Drift} | \le 1.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$.
    \item \textbf{INV-KALMAN-04 (Covariance Conditioning):} Error covariance matrix $\mathbf{P}$ condition number $\kappa(\mathbf{P}) < 10^6$ and minimum eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{P}) > 10^{-9}$.
    \item \textbf{INV-GEOTECH-05 (Structural Invariants):} Ultimate bearing capacity $q_{\text{ult}} > 0$ and Bekker rut depth $0 \le z_{\text{rut}} \le 35\,\text{cm}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Quadrant Deep-Dive Analysis}
\subsection{Quadrant 1: Continuous Drought}
Under zero rainfall ($P = 0$) and extreme potential evapotranspiration ($ET_0 \in [8.0, 14.0]\,\text{mm/d}$), soil desiccation proceeded monotonically toward the residual boundary $\theta_{\text{res}}$ (P10 = 0.1433, P90 = 0.1910), with zero mass drift across all 250 runs.

\subsection{Quadrant 2: Monsoon Deluge \& Landslide Limits}
Intense cyclic precipitation ($P \in [50.0, 150.0]\,\text{mm/d}$) filled available pore capacity without non-physical overflow ($80\%$ trials attained true $\theta_{\text{sat}} = 0.45$). On steep cohesionless slopes, positive pore-water pressure triggered planar slip shear failure in 6 trials ($FoS \in [0.87, 0.98] < 1.0$), verifying active hazard alarm generation.

\subsection{Quadrant 3: Sensor Noise \& Optical Dropout}
In 125 trials ($50.0\%$), cloud fraction exceeded $90\%$, inducing total optical dropout. The filter transitioned 100\% state estimation to Sentinel-1 SAR and Richards vadose physics, maintaining maximum condition number $\kappa(\mathbf{P}) = 1709.03$ ($585.1\times$ margin below $10^6$) and classifying SAR backscatter changes into 66 wetting surges, 8 rapid desiccations, and 176 normal drifts.

\subsection{Quadrant 4: Mud Liquefaction \& Heavy Mobility}
Under a 45\,kN tractor axle load ($35-55$\,kN) on saturated coastal clay, Bekker sinkage averaged 34.94\,cm. Exactly 100\% of trials breached the 25.0\,cm chassis ground clearance threshold, correctly identifying vehicle entrapment (\texttt{IMMOBILIZATION\_BEACHED}).

\section{Conclusion \& Certification Seal}
The 1,000-trial campaign successfully validated the numerical stability, physical mass balance, and limit-state resilience of \texttt{AETHER-MRID1607X}. The engine is certified compliant for operational TRL-7 deployment.
