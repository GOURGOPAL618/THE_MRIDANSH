## 📜 SECTION 1: OUR SOVEREIGN PLEDGE

As contributors, maintainers, researchers, and stewards of **THE MRIDANSH** and the **AETHER-MRID1607X** computational engine, we pledge to foster an environment characterized by:
1. **Uncompromised Scientific Honesty:** Absolute truthfulness in empirical reporting, mathematical modeling, and benchmark telemetry.
2. **Grassroots Benevolence (*Kisan Sewa*):** Prioritizing the survival, prosperity, and ecological security of smallholder farmers and agrarian communities across Bharat and the Global South.
3. **Mutual Respect & Dignity:** A welcoming, collegial, and safe space for every bona fide researcher, engineer, student, and collaborator regardless of background, gender, race, nationality, or institutional affiliation.
4. **Technological Dharma:** Engineering spaceborne and ground-observation algorithms that defend life, safeguard water tables, and eliminate artificial monopolization of Earth observation intelligence.

## 🛡️ SECTION 2: EXPECTED BEHAVIORS & SCIENTIFIC STANDARDS

All community members, contributors, reviewers, and maintainers are expected to uphold the highest standards of scientific professionalism and peer camaraderie:

### 2.1 Professionalism & Constructive Collaboration
- **Intellectual Humility & Respect:** Critique ideas, mathematical formulations, and engineering implementations constructively—never attack individuals or their origins.
- **Welcoming Diverse Perspectives:** Foster an open scientific dialogue where emerging researchers and students are mentored with patience and rigor.
- **Honoring Attribution:** Rigorously credit prior literature, datasets (ISRO Bhuvan, Copernicus Sentinel, USGS Landsat, SMAP), and JCC architectural foundations.
- **Clear & Documented Code:** Ensure all submitted pull requests and algorithms adhere to clean code principles, typed contracts, and self-documenting docstrings.

### 2.2 Unwavering Scientific Integrity (DO-178C Level-A Invariants)
- **Zero Data Fabrication:** Submitting forged test outputs, fabricated synthetic telemetry, or manipulated regression metrics is considered severe professional misconduct.
- **Strict Conservation Law Adherence:** Never intentionally bypass mass conservation checks (Richards PDE residuals $< 10^{-6}$), hydro-physical stability limits, or Kalman covariance conditioning.
- **Full Reproducibility:** Ensure all experimental findings, notebooks, and models include verifiable seeds, pipeline configs, and reproducible execution paths.
- **Safety-Critical Terramechanics:** Never falsify bearing capacity or slope stability safety factors ($\text{FS} \ge 1.30$) to misrepresent unstable or waterlogged soil as safe for heavy civil machinery.

## 🚫 SECTION 3: UNACCEPTABLE BEHAVIORS & ZERO-TOLERANCE VIOLATIONS

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited across all project channels, repositories, discussion forums, issues, pull requests, and associated academic meetings:

### 3.1 Harassment & Interpersonal Misconduct
- **Discriminatory Conduct:** Any offensive, derogatory, or exclusionary remarks concerning caste, religion, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or physical appearance.
- **Harassment & Stalking:** Unwelcome sexual attention, persistent uninvited private contact, intimidation, doxxing, or publishing another individual’s private information without explicit consent.
- **Trolling & Hostility:** Gratuitous insults, inflammatory political tirades, dismissive gatekeeping, or sustained disruption of technical discussions.

### 3.2 Technological Exploitation & Malice
- **Predatory Commercialization:** Attempting to monetize free grassroots farmer advisories, wrapping the open engine for proprietary paywalls, or violating the terms of [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) (`JSTIRPL-v1.0`).
- **Malicious Code Injection:** Knowingly introducing backdoor vectors, telemetry spyware, unverified non-deterministic dependencies, or obfuscated payloads into the flight code.
- **Surveillance & Kinetic Abuse:** Submitting features, pull requests, or configurations designed to enable offensive military targeting, weaponized crop destruction, or unauthorized population surveillance.

## 🚨 SECTION 5: REPORTING & ENFORCEMENT TRIBUNAL

### 5.1 How to Report an Incident
If you experience, witness, or suspect a violation of this Code of Conduct or scientific integrity standards:
1. **Direct Electronic Transmission:** Send a confidential detailed incident report to:
   - **Commander Gouragopal Mohapatra:** [`ggmohapatra.info.2007@gmail.com`](mailto:ggmohapatra.info.2007@gmail.com)
   - **Subject Line:** `[JCC-COC-INCIDENT] <Brief Description>`
2. **Required Details:** Include timestamps, links to issues/commits/pull requests, screenshots (if applicable), involved handles, and a factual chronological summary.
3. **Confidentiality Guarantee:** All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality to protect the reporter from retaliation.

### 5.2 Disciplinary Actions & Sovereign Enforcement
The Mission Commander and JCC Supreme Command Tribunal will investigate reports within 48 hours. Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include:

| Severity Level | Violation Nature | Enforcement Action |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Minor unprofessionalism, inappropriate language, or dismissive conduct in PR/issues. | **Official Written Reprimand:** Private clarification and request to amend conduct. |
| **Level 2** | Repeated incivility, targeted insults, trolling, or persistent refusal to follow review feedback. | **Temporary Suspension:** 14-day to 30-day block from repository interactions and discussions. |
| **Level 3** | Harassment, doxxing, discriminatory remarks, or intentional falsification of scientific test results. | **Indefinite Expulsion:** Permanent block from all JCC repositories, deletion of toxic contributions. |
| **Level 4** | Malicious backdoor injection, kinetic weaponization attempt, commercial IP theft, or legal intimidation of farmers. | **Immediate Permanent Ban + Legal Referral:** Permanent expulsion, public disclosure of bad actor, and legal referral to the **High Court of Orissa, Cuttack, Bharat** under Bharatiya Law. |
